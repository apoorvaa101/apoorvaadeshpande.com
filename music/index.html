Music

Vocalist | Composer | Performer

Apoorvaa is a versatile vocalist, a creative composer with an energizing stage presence. Building on her strong foundation in Indian classical music, Apoorvaa experiments across different genres of music to bring something fresh-yet-familiar for her audiences.

Her digital presence boasts of multiple popular videos with millions of views. She has been invited to perform globally at prestigious events such as BMM 2024, International Yoga Day (Tel Aviv), Montalvo Center of Arts, etc.

You can invite Apoorvaa to perform based on your unique occasion and audience. She has a huge range of repertoire as follows:

  • Themed concerts [Rasa & Raga]
  • Indian fusion music [ Jazz-fusion, Folk-fusion]
  • Traditional Hindustani classical concert [ Homestyle, Community-oriented, Pi-Baithak]
  • Pop songs [Bollywood, Medleys, Ghazals, Folk, Thumri, Marathi]
  • Devotional and spiritual [Bhajans, Nirguni, Abhangs]
  • Customized intimate concerts [A story of belonging]

Background

Apoorvaa has been fortunate to be born in a musical family and has been a student of music for 25+ years. She has been learning Hindustani classical music under the tutelage of her mother (Guru) Smt. Meenal Deshpande, a prominent vocalist of the Jaipur-Atrauli Gharana, who herself has been a disciple of Pandit (Late) Ratnakar Pai and Gaansaraswati Smt. (Late) Kishori Amonkar.

Apoorvaa has attained Sangeet Visharad from Gandharva Mahavidyalaya, equivalent to a bachelor’s degree in music. She is also a recipient of the prestigious CCRT (Centre for Cultural Resources & Training) scholarship by the Government of India for her music. She likes to present and promote Indian classical music through interactive talks.

IndianRaga

Apoorvaa is a senior fellow at IndianRaga. This prestigious fellowship is awarded to artists across the globe and across different genres of performances, selected by a panel of renowned international judges. As an IndianRaga fellow, Apoorvaa got to meet and collaborate with some amazing musicians, dancers and sound designers.

Some of her popular videos from the fellowship:

  • Padharo Mhare Des The quintessential Rajasthani folk song presented with a contemporary instrumentation
  • Malare| Abhi na jao| Poove Sempoove This is a multi lingual medley of three evergreen romantic songs, in a lovely waltz melody.
  • Swar Saadhana This is a contemporary Hindustani Carnatic Jugalbandi with a bandish in Raag Jog written and composed by Apoorvaa, and a krithi in Ragam Vaagadeeshwari.
  • Irish Malhar This is a unique piece that combines the beautiful Irish piece ‘Toss the Feathers’ by the band Corrs with a Hindustani traditional composition
  • Ghazal Transposed This is a novel experiment in which the iconic ghazal (Aaj jaane ki zidd na karo) is transposed in seven different Raag s

Latest

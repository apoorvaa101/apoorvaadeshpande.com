About

Apoorvaa is a PolyPath; she pursues diverse paths in life and loves to grow in multiple dimensions towards her truest self. Her life paths include tech, music, research, writing and parenting, and together they form her unique, interdisciplinary identity. Checkout the PolyPaths Substack.

On her tech path, Apoorvaa is a computer scientist by training (PhD in cryptography), and currently works at the intersection of generative AI X privacy tech. Using her expertise and passion, she innovates to build products and features with privacy at their core. Read more here.

On her musical path, Apoorvaa is a versatile vocalist, a creative composer with an energizing stage presence. She has a strong foundation in Indian classical music, and experiments across different genres of music to bring something fresh-yet-familiar for her audiences. Read more here.
